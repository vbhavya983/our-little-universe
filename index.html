CHAPTER 04 · COLLEGE

A whole new chapter.

Then college happened.

You in Gwalior. Me in Vidisha.

And suddenly, loving each other meant learning how to love from a distance.

But some of my favourite college memories are still the simplest ones:

Sneaking out of the hostel, having dinner with you, and then just walking on the roads together.

CHAPTER 05 · LONG DISTANCE

Miles apart, never really apart.

College gave us a distance we never asked for.

Sometimes the hardest part wasn't anything dramatic. It was simply feeling alone.

Wishing you were here. Wishing I could just hug you, sit beside you, and not have to say anything.

So we found our own little way of being together:

Video calls. Late conversations. Seeing each other's faces through a screen.

The distance gave us a lot of ups and downs. But somehow...

We won.

CHAPTER 06 · REUNION

And then, you're here again.

Long distance never meant forever apart.

Sometimes you come to meet me, and suddenly the distance doesn't matter anymore.

The calls become real. The screen disappears. And I can finally just be next to you again. ♡

CHAPTER 07 · TODAY

And here we are.

Today, we know each other in ways we never did before.

We know each other's families. Our goals. Our dreams. Our fears.

We're both working on our careers and trying our best to shape a future together.

We fight. A lot. 😂

But we love each other so, so, so much.

You're my best friend. My partner. My safe place.

And somehow, you're my everythingggg. ♡
